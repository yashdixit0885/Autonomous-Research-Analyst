Item 5.  Other Events.
- ------   ------------ 

     On April 17, 1995, the Board of Directors of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.
(the "Company") acting through its Stockholder Rights Committee, ordered the
redemption of the Company's preferred stock purchase rights (the "Rights"),
issued in February 1990 to holders of the Company's Common Stock, to occur on
May 3, 1995.  The redemption price of $.01 per Right will be payable on May 24,
1995 to holders of the Company's Common Stock as of May 3, 1995.
