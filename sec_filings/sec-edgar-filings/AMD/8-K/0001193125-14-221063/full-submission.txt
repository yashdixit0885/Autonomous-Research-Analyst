<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><B>AMD Announces Private Offering of $400 Million </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><B>of Senior Notes </B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>SUNNYVALE, Calif.
&#151; June&nbsp;2, 2014 </B>&#151; Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (NYSE: AMD) today announced that it intends to commence a private offering, subject to market and other conditions, of $400 million aggregate principal amount of senior notes due 2024.
AMD intends to use the net proceeds received in the offering (the &#147;Net Proceeds&#148;) to repurchase its outstanding 8.125% Senior Notes due 2017 (the &#147;8.125% Notes&#148;) through a tender offer which also launched today, June&nbsp;2, 2014
(the &#147;8.125% Tender Offer&#148;). AMD intends to use the Net Proceeds to fund the purchase of all 8.125% Notes that are early tendered (the &#147;Early Tender&#148;) in accordance with the terms of the 8.125% Tender Offer. </P>
