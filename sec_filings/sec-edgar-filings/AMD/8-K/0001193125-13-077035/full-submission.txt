resigned as a member of the Board of Directors (the &#147;Board&#148;) of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;). Mr.&nbsp;Al Muhairi was appointed to the Board pursuant to the terms of that certain Master Transaction Agreement,
dated October&nbsp;6, 2008, with Advanced Technology Investment Corporation LLC and West Coast Hitech, L.P. (&#147;WCH&#148;), the Company&#146;s largest stockholder, acting through West Coast Hitech G.P., Ltd., its general partner, which was
further amended on December&nbsp;5, 2008. The Master Transaction Agreement provides that, until such time as WCH and its permitted transferees beneficially own, in the aggregate, less than 10% of the outstanding shares of the Company&#146;s common
stock, WCH has the right to designate a representative to the Board. For more information regarding the Master Transaction Agreement and other transactions with WCH and its affiliates, please see the Company&#146;s Current Report on Form 8-K, filed
on November&nbsp;8, 2012. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">On February&nbsp;22, 2013, WCH designated Martin L. Edelman to fill the vacancy created by
Mr.&nbsp;Muhairi&#146;s resignation, and the Board appointed Mr.&nbsp;Edelman as a director. The Board has deemed that Mr.&nbsp;Edelman is not independent and has not appointed Mr.&nbsp;Edelman to any committees of the Board. Mr.&nbsp;Edelman will
receive similar compensation as the Company provides to non-employee directors, which is described in the Company&#146;s definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March&nbsp;15, 2012, including a grant of 30,080
restricted stock units, all of which will vest on the first anniversary of the date of grant. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Mr.&nbsp;Craig&nbsp;A. Conway,
who is currently a member of the Board, will not stand for re-election to the Board at the Company&#146;s 2013 Annual Meeting of Stockholders. Mr.&nbsp;Conway has decided not to stand for re-election in order to focus on his other business and
personal interests. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">A copy of the press release announcing these items is attached as Exhibit 99.1 hereto. </FONT></P>
