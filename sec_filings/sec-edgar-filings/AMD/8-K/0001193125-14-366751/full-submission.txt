<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B><I>Departure of Directors or Certain Officers </I></B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On October&nbsp;8, 2014, Advanced Micro Devices (the &#147;<B><I>Company</I></B>&#148;) announced that Rory P. Read had stepped down from his
positions as the Company&#146;s President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Company&#146;s Board of Directors (the &#147;<B><I>Board</I></B>&#148;). The Board and Mr.&nbsp;Read are currently negotiating a Transition, Separation
Agreement and Release. Effective immediately, Mr.&nbsp;Read will continue his employment with the Company in a non-executive role as an advisor through December&nbsp;31, 2014, when his employment with the Company will end. Mr.&nbsp;Read will
continue to receive the salary and benefits he currently receives during this period of time. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:18pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B><I>Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain
Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers </I></B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On October&nbsp;8, 2014, the Board appointed Dr.&nbsp;Lisa&nbsp;T. Su as
the Company&#146;s President and Chief Executive Officer and as a director of the Company. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Dr.&nbsp;Su, 44, joined the Company in January
2012 and has served as Senior Vice President and Chief Operating Officer since July&nbsp;1, 2014. From January 2012 until July&nbsp;1, 2014, Dr.&nbsp;Su served as Senior Vice President and General Manager, Global Business Units. Before joining the
Company, Dr.&nbsp;Su served as Senior Vice President and General Manager, Networking and Multimedia at Freescale Semiconductor, Inc., a company that designs and manufactures embedded processors, where she was responsible for global strategy,
marketing, product management and engineering for Freescale&#146;s embedded communications and applications processor businesses.&nbsp;Dr.&nbsp;Su joined Freescale in 2007 as Chief Technology Officer, where she led the company&#146;s technology
roadmap and research and development efforts. She was promoted to Senior Vice President and General Manager, Networking and Multimedia in September 2008.&nbsp;Before her employment with Freescale, Dr.&nbsp;Su spent 13 years with IBM in various
engineering and business leadership positions, including Vice President of IBM&#146;s Semiconductor Research and Development Center, responsible for the strategic direction of IBM&#146;s silicon technologies, joint development alliances and
semiconductor R&amp;D operations. Dr.&nbsp;Su has served on the board of directors of Analog Devices since June 2012. Dr.&nbsp;Su holds a bachelor&#146;s, master&#146;s and doctorate degrees in electrical engineering from the Massachusetts Institute
of Technology (MIT), has been published in more than 40 technical publications and was named a Fellow of the Institute of Electronics and Electrical Engineers (IEEE) in 2009. Dr.&nbsp;Su was also named in MIT Technology Review&#146;s Top 100 Young
Innovators in 2002. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Dr. Su and the Board are currently negotiating the terms of an employment agreement. Dr.&nbsp;Su is currently paid an
annual base salary of $650,000. Further details of Dr.&nbsp;Su&#146;s compensation are set forth in the Company&#146;s proxy statement for its Annual Meeting of Stockholders, which was filed with the Securities Exchange Commission on March&nbsp;25,
2014 and is incorporated by reference herein. </P>
