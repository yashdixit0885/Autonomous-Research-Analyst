 <P STYLE="margin-top:0px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B>Item&nbsp;1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. </B></FONT></P> <P
STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">On December&nbsp;22, 2006, the New York State Urban Development Corporation d/b/a Empire State Development Corporation (the
&#147;<B><I>ESDC</I></B>&#148;) and Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;<B><I>Company</I></B>&#148;) entered into a Grant Disbursement Agreement (the &#147;<B><I>Agreement</I></B>&#148;) whereby the State of New York, within 90 days after the
Company notifies ESDC of its plans to initiate the construction of a new 300 mm wafer fabrication facility (the &#147;<B><I>Facility</I></B>&#148;) on the Luther Forest Technology Campus in Saratoga County, New York (the
&#147;<B><I>Project</I></B>&#148;), is required to issue bonds or otherwise fund the Project and related research and development in the amount of $650,000,000 (the &#147;<B><I>Grant</I></B>&#148;). Actual disbursement of such funds to the Company
will occur as the Company submits appropriate documentation verifying that expenditures on the Project have been incurred. Pursuant to the Agreement, the Company may give notice to ESDC to commence the Project anytime between June 2007 and July
2009, but the Company is not obligated to ever give notice. If the Company does not notify ESDC of its intent to move forward with construction of the Project by July&nbsp;31, 2009, the Agreement will terminate without penalty to the Company.
</FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">If the Company moves forward with the Project, the Company must complete the construction of the Facility in accordance with the final
plans and specifications approved in writing by ESDC and must maintain its business operations on the Luther Forest Technology Campus for a minimum of seven years after the date full employment of the Facility is first obtained. Funds disbursed to
the Company may be subject to repayment, in whole or part, if the Company does not attain and or maintain certain levels of employment for specified periods of time. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT
FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Pursuant to the terms of the Agreement, ESDC may terminate the Agreement if an Event of Default is not cured by the Company within 60 days after ESDC notifies the Company of such Event of Default. An &#147;<B><I>Event
of Default</I></B>&#148; is defined as, among other things, (i)&nbsp;any breach of the Company&#146;s representations and warranties in the Agreement, (ii)&nbsp;the failure of the Company to comply with any request for information by ESDC,
(iii)&nbsp;a material breach by the Company of the Agreement, (iv)&nbsp;the failure to complete the Project by December&nbsp;31, 2014, (v)&nbsp;the failure to carry on the design and construction of the Project with reasonable dispatch or the
discontinued design and construction of the Project for a period of 60 days, without proper notification being made to the ESDC, (vi)&nbsp;the sale, conveyance, assignment or transfer by the Company of the Facility without the consent of ESDC,
(vii)&nbsp;the failure by the Company to pay any indebtedness when due or (vii)&nbsp;the filing of bankruptcy by the Company. Upon termination of the Agreement due to an Event of Default, ESDC may withhold any portion of the Grant not disbursed to
the Company and require repayment by the Company of any portions of the Grant previously disbursed to the Company. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">A copy of the Agreement
is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference. The foregoing description of the Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of this Agreement. </FONT></P> <P
