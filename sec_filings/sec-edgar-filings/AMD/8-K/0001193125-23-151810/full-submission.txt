 <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><span style="font-weight:bold">Item 5.02</span> <span style="font-weight:bold">Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers</span>. </p> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On May&#160;18, 2023, Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#8220;Company&#8221;) held its 2023 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the &#8220;Annual Meeting&#8221;). At the Annual Meeting, the Company&#8217;s stockholders approved the Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. 2023 Equity Incentive Plan (the &#8220;2023 Plan&#8221;), which replaces the Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. 2004 Equity Incentive Plan and the Xilinx, Inc. 2007 Equity Incentive Plan (the &#8220;Prior Plans&#8221;). Outstanding awards granted under the Prior Plans will continue to be governed by the terms of the Prior Plans but no awards may be made under the Prior Plans on or after May 18, 2023. Under the 2023 Plan, 87,645,874 shares of the Company&#8217;s common stock are reserved and available for delivery pursuant to awards granted under the 2023 Plan. Officers, directors, employees, and consultants who provide services to the Company or any subsidiary are eligible to participate in the 2023 Plan. </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">A description of the material terms of the 2023 Plan was included in the Company&#8217;s Proxy Statement on Schedule 14A filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March&#160;31, 2023, and mailed to the Company&#8217;s stockholders commencing on or about March&#160;31, 2023 in connection with the Annual Meeting (the &#8220;Proxy Statement&#8221;), and is incorporated by reference into this Item 5.02 of this Current Report on Form <span style="white-space:nowrap">8-K.</span> The foregoing summary is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the 2023 Plan, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form <span style="white-space:nowrap">8-K</span> and is incorporated herein by reference. </p> <p style="margin-top:18pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold">Item&#160;5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. </p> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">At the Annual Meeting, the Company&#8217;s stockholders voted on the following five proposals, each of which is described in detail in the Company&#8217;s Proxy Statement: </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold">Proposal No.&#160;1: Election of Directors - All Directors Re-Elected. </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The following individuals were elected to the Company&#8217;s Board of Directors: </p> <p style="font-size:12pt;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:0pt">&#160;</p>
