of time-based stock options (with a grant date fair value equal to 25% of the Award Value), time-based restricted stock units (with a grant date fair value equal to 25% of the Award Value) and performance-based restricted stock units (with a grant
date fair value equal to 50% of the Award Value) using a $4.10 share price for the time-based stock options, time-based restricted stock units and performance-based restricted stock units and a 38.86% binominal factor for the time-based stock
options. These awards will be granted on August&nbsp;12, 2014 under the Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. 2004 Equity Incentive Plan, as amended and restated. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><I><U>Time-Based Stock Options</U></I>. The time-based stock options will have an exercise price equal to 100% of the fair market value of the
Company&#146;s common stock on the grant date and will vest 33<SUP STYLE="vertical-align:top">&nbsp;1</SUP>&#8260;<SUB STYLE="vertical-align:bottom">3</SUB>% on August&nbsp;12, 2015 and 8<SUP STYLE="vertical-align:top">&nbsp;1</SUP>&#8260;<SUB
STYLE="vertical-align:bottom">3</SUB>%&nbsp;per quarter over the next eight following quarters. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><I><U>Time-Based RSUs</U></I>. The
time-based restricted stock units will vest 33<SUP STYLE="vertical-align:top">&nbsp;1</SUP>&#8260;<SUB STYLE="vertical-align:bottom">3</SUB>% on each of August&nbsp;9, 2015,&nbsp;2016 and 2017. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><I><U>Performance-Based RSUs</U></I>. The initial number of performance-based restricted stock units that may be earned will be based upon the
Company achieving a certain pre-established target level of adjusted non-GAAP operating income plus interest expense over a two-year performance period commencing on January&nbsp;1, 2014 and ending on December&nbsp;31, 2015 (the &#147;<U>Performance
Period</U>&#148;). Once the initial award amount is determined, the performance-based restricted stock units will then be subject to adjustment based upon a second metric, the Company&#146;s total shareholder return (&#147;<U>TSR</U>&#148;) relative
to the TSR of the S&amp;P 500 IT Sector over the Performance Period. If the Company&#146;s TSR for the Performance Period is at or above the 75<SUP STYLE="font-size:85%; vertical-align:top">th</SUP>&nbsp;percentile, then the Named Executive Officer
will earn 125% of the initial number of shares. If the Company&#146;s TSR for the Performance Period is above the 25<SUP STYLE="font-size:85%; vertical-align:top">th</SUP>&nbsp;percentile and below the
75<SUP STYLE="font-size:85%; vertical-align:top">th</SUP>&nbsp;percentile, a proportionate adjustment between 75% and 125% is applied to the initial number of shares based on relative performance between the 25<SUP
STYLE="font-size:85%; vertical-align:top">th</SUP>&nbsp;and 75<SUP STYLE="font-size:85%; vertical-align:top">th</SUP>&nbsp;percentile. </P>
