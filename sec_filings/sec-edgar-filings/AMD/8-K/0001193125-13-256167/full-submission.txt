 <P STYLE="margin-top:0px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B>Item 5.02.&nbsp;Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain
Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. </B></FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B>Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers </B></FONT></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">On June 7, 2013, Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;) adopted the Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. Executive Severance Plan
for Senior Vice Presidents (the &#147;Plan&#148;) in order to implement a uniform process for handling potential future departures of senior executives. The primary purpose of the Plan is to provide separation pay to the Company&#146;s senior vice
presidents in the United States (each, a &#147;Participant&#148;). </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">The Named Executive Officers (as defined in the
Company&#146;s Proxy Statement for the Annual Meeting of Stockholders held on May 16, 2013), other than the Company&#146;s Chief Executive Officer, participate in the Plan. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT
STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Subject to the terms of the Plan, any Participant who (i) is involuntarily terminated from the Company other than for Cause (as defined below) or as a result of death or disability (as defined in the
Plan) and (ii) is not offered a job with an affiliate of the Company or a successor of the Company, shall be entitled to the following benefits: </FONT></P> <P STYLE="font-size:6px;margin-top:0px;margin-bottom:0px">&nbsp;</P>

<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top"> <P ALIGN="left"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">A single lump sum severance payment equivalent to twelve (12) months of base pay; </FONT></P></TD></TR></TABLE>
<P STYLE="font-size:6px;margin-top:0px;margin-bottom:0px">&nbsp;</P>

<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top"> <P ALIGN="left"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Payment by the Company of twelve (12) months of COBRA medical, dental and/or vision insurance premiums, based on the Participant&#146;s benefits plan
elections in effect at the time of termination; and </FONT></P></TD></TR></TABLE> <P STYLE="font-size:6px;margin-top:0px;margin-bottom:0px">&nbsp;</P>

<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top"> <P ALIGN="left"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Use of the Employee Assistance Plan provided by the Company as part of the twelve (12) months of COBRA coverage. </FONT></P></TD></TR></TABLE>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Under the terms of the Plan, &#147;Cause&#148; is defined as, among other things, (i)&nbsp;an act or omission by a Participant resulting
in him or her being charged with a criminal offense involving moral turpitude, dishonesty, or breach of trust; (ii)&nbsp;conduct by a Participant which constitutes a felony, plea of guilty or nolo contendere with respect to a felony under applicable
law; (iii)&nbsp;conduct by a Participant that constitutes gross neglect; (iv)&nbsp;a Participant&#146;s insubordination or refusal to implement directives of his or her manager; (v) a Participant&#146;s failure to substantially perform his or her
job duties with the Company; or (vi) a Participant&#146;s poor performance of his or her job duties as determined in the sole discretion of the Company. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT
STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">The Participant&#146;s receipt of the above severance benefits is subject to the Participant executing a general release. Additionally, the Company will set off on a pro-rata basis any severance benefits
in the event a Participant becomes re-employed by the Company during the 12-months following receipt of a severance payment under the Plan. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT
STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">This Plan is intended to represent the exclusive severance benefits payable to a Participant by the Company. Accordingly, any Participant who is entitled to receive severance benefits payable in
connection with a change of control of the Company pursuant to a change in </FONT></P>

benefits under a change in control agreement between the Participant and the Company. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Although the Company expects to
maintain the Plan indefinitely, the Company reserves the right to amend, modify or terminate the Plan at any time. Therefore, entitlement to severance benefits under the Plan are not guaranteed and may be eliminated in the future. </FONT></P>
