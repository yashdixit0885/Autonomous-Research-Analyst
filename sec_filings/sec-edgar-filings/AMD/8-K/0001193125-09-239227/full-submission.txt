<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B>Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
</B></FONT></TD></TR></TABLE> <P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">As previously reported, on January&nbsp;16, 2009, the Compensation Committee (the &#147;Compensation
Committee&#148;) of the Board of Directors of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;) approved across-the-board reductions to annual base salaries, including the annual base salaries of all named executive officers, effective as of
February&nbsp;1, 2009. Effective November&nbsp;16, 2009, the Compensation Committee approved the reversal of these reductions commencing on November 23, 2009. As a result, the reinstated annual base salaries of the named executive officers will be
the following: (i)&nbsp;Mr.&nbsp;Derrick Meyer, the Company&#146;s President and Chief Executive Officer, reinstated from $720,000 to $900,000; (ii)&nbsp;Mr.&nbsp;Robert Rivet, the Company&#146;s Executive Vice President, Chief Operations and
Administrative Officer, reinstated from $552,500 to $650,000; and (iii)&nbsp;Mr.&nbsp;Thomas McCoy, the Company&#146;s Executive Vice President, Legal, Corporate and Public Affairs, reinstated from $462,400 to $544,000. Because annual bonus targets
are a percentage of annual base salary, the across-the-board increases, in effect, would also increase fiscal 2009 annual bonus payments, if any. </FONT></P>
