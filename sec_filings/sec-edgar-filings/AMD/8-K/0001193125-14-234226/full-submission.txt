ANNOUNCES LATEST STEP IN MULTI-YEAR STRATEGIC TRANSFORMATION </B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><I>&#151;Dr. Lisa Su Named Chief Operating Officer; Company to Create Two
Business Groups Focused on Further Strengthening Traditional Businesses and Driving Growth in Adjacent Markets; Chief Sales Officer John Byrne to Lead New Computing and Graphics Business Group &#151; </I></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>SUNNYVALE CA &#151; June&nbsp;12, 2014 &#151;</B> <U>AMD</U> (NYSE: AMD) today announced the latest step in the company&#146;s multi-year
strategic transformation to deliver consistent growth and profitability by combining the company&#146;s previously separate business units, global operations and sales organizations to create a singular, market-focused organization responsible for
all aspects of product strategy, product execution, sales and operations. The new structure will be implemented on July&nbsp;1 and will be led by <U>Dr.&nbsp;Lisa Su</U> (44)&nbsp;in her new role as AMD&#146;s Chief Operating Officer (COO),
reporting to AMD President and Chief Executive Officer <U>Rory Read</U> (52). </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">As a part of the re-alignment, AMD also announced it will
consolidate its business units into two expanded business groups designed to deliver unmatched customer value in both traditional PC markets and adjacent high-growth markets: </P> <P STYLE="font-size:6pt;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:0pt">&nbsp;</P>
<TABLE STYLE="BORDER-COLLAPSE:COLLAPSE; font-family:Times New Roman; font-size:10pt" BORDER="0" CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%">

<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top">The Computing and Graphics Business Group will combine AMD&#146;s client, consumer graphics and professional graphics businesses, as well as their related product engineering and sales functions. AMD Chief Sales Officer
<U>John Byrne</U> (43)&nbsp;has been appointed senior vice president and general manager responsible for leading the Computing and Graphics Business Group, reporting to Dr.&nbsp;Su. </TD></TR></TABLE>

reset, accelerate and ultimately transform AMD,&#148; said Read. &#147;Today&#146;s announcement represents the next step in our long-term strategic plan to help ensure AMD&#146;s operating structure and culture are better aligned to drive
consistent growth and profitability by leveraging our leadership IP to create differentiated products that help our customers win across a diversified set of high-growth markets. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">&#147;Lisa has been a driving force in AMD&#146;s recent success, and as COO she will expand on this foundation and lead a broader
organization designed to more quickly adapt to industry shifts, streamline execution and decision making, and create even greater value for our customers. John&#146;s years of PC industry experience and successful track record of strengthening
AMD&#146;s customer relationships make him ideally suited to take on additional responsibility and lead one of our new business groups.&#148; </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Dr.&nbsp;Su joined AMD in 2012 as senior vice president and general manager, Global Business Units, responsible for the company&#146;s product
strategy, product definition and business plans. She joined AMD from Freescale, where she held multiple positions including chief technology officer and senior vice present and general manager, Networking and Multimedia. Prior to Freescale,
Dr.&nbsp;Su spent 13 years with IBM in various engineering and business leadership positions. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">During Byrne&#146;s seven years at AMD he
has held several senior management roles, including responsibility for global sales teams, leading the consolidation of the company&#146;s computing and graphics processor worldwide channel sales teams and strengthening relationships with the
company&#146;s largest global customers. He has more than 25 years of experience in the technology industry, including founding two successful technology sales and marketing businesses. </P>
