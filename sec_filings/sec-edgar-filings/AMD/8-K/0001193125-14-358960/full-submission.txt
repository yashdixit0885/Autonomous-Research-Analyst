 <P STYLE="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>Item&nbsp;5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain
Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. </B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On September&nbsp;24, 2014, the Compensation Committee of the Board of
Directors of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;<U>Company</U>&#148;) approved the following retention awards with respect to John Byrne, the Company&#146;s Senior Vice President and General Manager, Computing and Graphics Business Group, which
the Company will grant on October&nbsp;15, 2014. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><U><I>Cash Award</I><I>.</I></U> A time-based cash retention award of $1,000,000 will be
paid in two tranches. Half of the award will be paid on the first regular payroll date after September&nbsp;30, 2015, and the remainder will be paid on the first regular payroll date after September&nbsp;30, 2016. The award will accelerate and be
paid on the first regular payroll date after such acceleration if Mr.&nbsp;Byrne is involuntarily terminated by the Company other than for cause and if certain other conditions are met. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><U><I>Performance-Based RSU Award</I><I>.</I></U> Mr.&nbsp;Byrne will also be granted a performance-based restricted stock unit
(&#147;<U>PRSU</U>&#148;) retention award with an aggregate grant date fair value (the &#147;<U>Award Value</U>&#148;) of $1,000,000. The number of PRSUs granted will be determined by dividing the Award Value by the 30-day trailing average closing
price of the Company&#146;s common stock on the grant date. The PRSUs will only be earned to the extent certain pre-determined performance conditions are met. Half of the earned PRSUs will vest on September&nbsp;30, 2015, and the remainder will vest
on September&nbsp;30, 2016. The PRSUs will accelerate and vest immediately if Mr.&nbsp;Byrne is involuntarily terminated by the Company other than for cause and if certain other conditions are met. </P>
