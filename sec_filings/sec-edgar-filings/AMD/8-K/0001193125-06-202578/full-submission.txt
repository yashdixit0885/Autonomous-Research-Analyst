FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">On September&nbsp;28, 2006, the Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;) approved the offer letter with David Orton to serve as an Executive Vice
President of the Company, to be effective upon the closing (&#147;Closing&#148;) of the Company&#146;s acquisition of all of the outstanding common shares of ATI Technologies Inc. (&#147;ATI&#148;), as contemplated by the Acquisition Agreement dated
as of July&nbsp;23, 2006, among the Company, a Delaware corporation, 1252986 Alberta ULC, an unlimited liability company formed under the laws of Alberta and an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, and ATI, a corporation continued under
the laws of Canada. The offer letter supersedes any other ATI agreement with respect to Mr.&nbsp;Orton&#146;s employment with ATI. The offer letter provides for base compensation of $550,000 per year. In addition, the offer letter provides that
Mr.&nbsp;Orton is eligible to participate in (i)&nbsp;the Company&#146;s Corporate Bonus Plan, in which Mr.&nbsp;Orton&#146;s initial target bonus will be 100% of base compensation (&#147;Corporate Bonus&#148;) and (ii)&nbsp;the Company&#146;s Vice
President Long Term Incentive Plan, in which Mr.&nbsp;Orton&#146;s target payout is 30% of his base salary, based on rolling three-year revenue growth, relative to external benchmarks and operating income margins. Mr.&nbsp;Orton will also receive
two separate cash bonuses, each equal to $412,500, as an incentive bonus for his anticipated contributions in ensuring the effective integration of ATI&#146;s operations into AMD (&#147;Contribution Bonus&#148;). The first such bonus payment will be
paid to Mr.&nbsp;Orton in the first payroll cycle after one year of service with the Company and the second such bonus payment will be paid to Mr.&nbsp;Orton in the first payroll cycle after two years of service with the Company, provided, that
Mr.&nbsp;Orton is actively employed full-time at the time of payment with satisfactory job performance. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Mr.&nbsp;Orton will continue to
participate in the benefits plans, including any executive plans, that are made available by ATI, with the exception of ATI&#146;s 401(k) plan. Mr.&nbsp;Orton will be eligible to participate in the Company&#146;s 401(k) retirement savings plan with
matching contributions. The Company anticipates that Mr.&nbsp;Orton will discontinue his participation in the ATI benefit plans and be eligible to participate in the Company&#146;s benefits, perquisites and profit sharing plan beginning in January
2007. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Mr.&nbsp;Orton will also be granted 50,000 restricted stock units to vest 40% one year from the grant date, with an additional 5% at
the end of each subsequent three-month period, assuming continuous service. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">In the event that Mr.&nbsp;Orton&#146;s employment is
terminated by mutual agreement or in the event that Mr.&nbsp;Orton is terminated without cause during the first 12 months of employment following the Closing, Mr.&nbsp;Orton will be provided with 24 months of severance (&#147;Severance
Period&#148;). In the event that Mr.&nbsp;Orton&#146;s employment is terminated by mutual agreement or without cause between the 13th and 24th month of employment following the Closing, the Severance Period will be reduced by one month for each
month of service completed during this period. Mr.&nbsp;Orton&#146;s severance will include (i)&nbsp;base salary, (ii)&nbsp;Corporate Bonus at target and (iii)&nbsp;pro-rated payment of his Contribution Bonus at target for the applicable fiscal
period between the Closing and the termination of employment. Mr.&nbsp;Orton will also be eligible for vesting of his prior granted ATI equity (converted into AMD equity at Closing) that would vest during the applicable Severance Period.
Mr.&nbsp;Orton will also be eligible for medical and dental insurance continuance and outplacement assistance. </FONT></P>
