<P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B>SUNNYVALE, Calif. &#151; July&nbsp;26, 2010 &#151; </B>Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (NYSE: AMD) today announced that it has agreed to sell $500
million aggregate principal amount of its 7.75% Senior Notes due 2020 in a private offering. AMD intends to close the transaction on or around August&nbsp;4, 2010. </FONT></P>
