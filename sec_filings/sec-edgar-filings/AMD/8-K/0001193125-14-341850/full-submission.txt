<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Effective September&nbsp;15, 2014, the Board of Directors (the &#147;Board&#148;) of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;)
increased the size of the Board to twelve directors and appointed Mr.&nbsp;Joseph&nbsp;A. Householder as an independent director to the Board. In addition, Mr.&nbsp;Householder was appointed to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee and
the Audit and Finance Committee of the Board. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Mr.&nbsp;Householder is entitled to annual cash retainer fees of $75,000 for his services
as a director, $10,000 for his services on the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee and $20,000 for his services on the Audit and Finance Committee. These retainer fees will be pro-rated for the 2014 calendar year based on the date of his
appointment to the Board and the Board committees. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In addition, pursuant to the Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. Outside Director Equity
Compensation Policy (the &#147;Policy&#148;), on September&nbsp;15, 2014, Mr.&nbsp;Householder was granted a restricted stock unit award for 44,589 shares of Company common stock, which is equal to the quotient of (i)&nbsp;$185,000 divided by
(ii)&nbsp;the average closing price of the Company&#146;s common stock for the 30-day period immediately preceding and ending as of Mr.&nbsp;Householder&#146;s appointment to the Board (rounded down to the nearest whole number). Pursuant to and
subject to the terms of the Policy, the grant becomes fully vested on the one-year anniversary of the grant date. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">A copy of the press

<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><B>AMD Appoints Joseph A. Householder to Board of Directors </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>SUNNYVALE, Calif. &#151; SEPT. 15, 2014 </B>&#151; AMD (NYSE: AMD) today announced the appointment of Joseph A. Householder, 59, to its
<U>board of directors</U> along with positions on the board&#146;s Audit and Finance, and Nominating and Corporate Governance committees. Householder currently serves as executive vice president and chief financial officer of Sempra Energy. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Householder possesses significant financial and operations expertise from his 13 years at Sempra Energy, in addition to former leadership
roles at PricewaterhouseCoopers and Unocal Corporation. He currently serves on the board of directors of multiple wholly- or majority-owned subsidiaries of Sempra Energy, including the Southern California Gas Company, San Diego Gas&nbsp;&amp;
