 <P STYLE="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>Item&nbsp;5.02.&nbsp;Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain
Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. </B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>Compensatory Arrangements of the President and Chief Executive Officer </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">At its meeting on August&nbsp;6, 2014, the Compensation Committee (the &#147;Compensation Committee&#148;) of the Board of Directors (the
&#147;Board&#148;) of Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;) approved annual equity award grants to Dr.&nbsp;Lisa Su, which reflected her promotion to Senior Vice President and Chief Operating Officer on July&nbsp;1, 2014. In October
2014, the Company entered into an employment agreement with Dr.&nbsp;Su in connection with her promotion to President and Chief Executive Officer. As negotiated and provided for in Dr.&nbsp;Su&#146;s employment agreement, Dr.&nbsp;Su received
additional equity grants on October&nbsp;31, 2014, in light of her appointment to the new position. The employment agreement was approved by the Board, the terms and conditions of which were the result of arms-length negotiations between Dr.&nbsp;Su
and the Compensation Committee. The Company believes that Dr.&nbsp;Su&#146;s compensation package, as provided for in her employment agreement, was appropriate and aligned with the Company&#146;s stockholders&#146; interests. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On November&nbsp;24, 2014, the Company received notice of a stockholder derivative action in Delaware court on the grounds that the Company
had granted equity awards to Dr.&nbsp;Su during calendar year 2014 in excess of the per calendar year individual share limit in the Company&#146;s 2004 Equity Incentive Plan (as amended, the &#147;2004 Plan&#148;). Rather than litigate this
technical issue, the Company believes resolving this technicality quickly is a better solution for the Company and its stockholders. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In
light of this determination, on December&nbsp;26, 2014, the full Board, including the members of the Compensation Committee, took the following actions with respect to Dr.&nbsp;Su&#146;s equity compensation: </P>
<P STYLE="font-size:6pt;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:0pt">&nbsp;</P>

subject to such award. </TD></TR></TABLE> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In voiding and rescinding the equity awards described above, the full Board also determined that the
total compensation package provided for in Dr.&nbsp;Su&#146;s employment agreement, including the equity compensation, was appropriate and aligned with stockholders&#146; interests. Having reaffirmed that the compensation it had promised to
Dr.&nbsp;Su was appropriate and reasonable, the full Board determined that the Company intends to return Dr.&nbsp;Su&#146;s equity compensation to the level it should have been prior to the action to void and rescind the equity awards described
above at or near the earliest practicable opportunity available to the Company, subject to law and the terms of the 2004 Plan. </P>
