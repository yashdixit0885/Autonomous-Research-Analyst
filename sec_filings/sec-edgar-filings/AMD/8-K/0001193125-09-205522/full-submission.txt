Company (the &#147;<B>Offer Letter</B>&#148;), Mr.&nbsp;Seifert&#146;s annual base salary will be $525,000. Mr.&nbsp;Seifert is also eligible for an annual performance bonus under the Company&#146;s Executive Incentive Plan in a target amount of
150% of his base salary, to be payable upon his achievement of certain performance goals and objectives to be determined by the Company&#146;s Board of Directors. Mr.&nbsp;Seifert will also receive an option to purchase 250,000 shares (the
&#147;<B>Shares</B>&#148;) of the Company&#146;s common stock at an exercise price equal to the closing price of the Company&#146;s common stock on the date of the grant. The Shares will vest over a&nbsp;36-month period from date of grant: 33.3% on
November&nbsp;15, 2010 and then 8.3% every three months over the next 24 months, assuming Mr.&nbsp;Seifert&#146;s continuous active service with the Company. In addition, Mr.&nbsp;Seifert will be granted&nbsp;125,000 restricted stock units (the
&#147;<B>RSUs</B>&#148;).&nbsp;Notwithstanding the vesting information contained in the Offer Letter, the&nbsp;RSUs will vest 33.3% on each of November&nbsp;9, 2010,&nbsp;November&nbsp;9, 2011 and November&nbsp;9, 2012, assuming
Mr.&nbsp;Seifert&#146;s continuous active service with the Company. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px; text-indent:4%;padding-bottom:3px;"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Pursuant to a relocation expenses

(the &#147;<B>Sign-On Bonus Agreement</B>&#148;), the Company will reimburse Mr.&nbsp;Seifert for certain relocation expenses incurred by Mr.&nbsp;Seifert and pay Mr.&nbsp;Seifert a one-time sign-on bonus of $150,000 in connection with his
employment with the Company. In the event Mr.&nbsp;Seifert&#146;s employment with the Company is terminated within 13 months of the Effective Date, Mr.&nbsp;Seifert will repay all such relocation expenses and the sign-on bonus to the Company. In the
event Mr.&nbsp;Seifert&#146;s employment with the Company is terminated after 13 months of the Effective Date but less than 24 months after the Effective Date, Mr.&nbsp;Seifert will repay all such relocation expenses to the Company and the sign-on
bonus, less 8.33% of such relocation expenses and sign-on bonus for each full month of employment completed after the 12</FONT><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="1"><SUP
